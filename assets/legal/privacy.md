# Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Wide-Mart ("we", "us") is run by [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS], Uganda. This policy explains what personal data the Wide-Mart app collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and its Regulations.

Questions or requests: [PRIVACY EMAIL]

## 1. What we collect

**Data you give us**
- Account details: name, email address, phone number, district and area, password (stored only in encrypted form by our login provider).
- Profile photo, if you add one.
- Ads you post: title, description, price, condition, location and photos.
- Job posts and job applications, including the CV and cover letter you upload.
- Chat messages you send to other users.
- Reviews and reports you write.
- Verification documents (ID photo and selfie), only if you ask to be verified.

**Data created when you use the app**
- Payment records for boosts and featured jobs: amount, plan, reference and status. Card and mobile money details are handled by our payment partner, Flutterwave. We never see or store your card number or PIN.
- A device token so we can send you push notifications.
- Crash reports and app usage events (for example "ad viewed" or "search made"), linked to an account number, not your name or phone.

**Data from sign-in partners**
- If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and profile photo from Google.
- If you sign in with a WhatsApp or SMS code, we receive your phone number.

## 2. Why we use it
- To create and secure your account.
- To show your ads, job posts and profile to other users.
- To let buyers, sellers, employers and applicants contact each other.
- To check ads before they go live and to act on reports of fraud or abuse.
- To verify sellers who ask for a verified badge.
- To process payments and keep payment records.
- To send notifications you can turn off in your phone settings.
- To find and fix crashes and improve the app.
- To meet our legal duties.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. What other users can see
- Your name, profile photo, district, verified badge, rating and reviews.
- Your ads and job posts, including the location you enter.
- Your phone number, on the ads and jobs you post, so people can call or WhatsApp you. Do not post an ad if you do not want your number shown.
- Employers see the CV and cover letter you send them when you apply to their job.

Your email address, verification documents and payment records are never shown to other users.

## 4. Who we share data with
We use these service providers to run the app. They process data only for us.
- **Supabase**: database, login and file storage.
- **Google Firebase**: push notifications, crash reports and usage statistics.
- **Google**: "Sign in with Google".
- **Twilio and WhatsApp (Meta)**: sending sign-in codes.
- **Flutterwave**: processing payments.

We may also share data when the law requires it, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

## 5. Transfers outside Uganda
Some of these providers store data on servers outside Uganda. Where this happens, we transfer data only as allowed by the Data Protection and Privacy Act and with safeguards that give protection at least equal to Ugandan law.

## 6. How long we keep data
- Account data: while your account is open.
- When you delete your account, we delete your profile, ads, photos, job posts, applications, CVs, messages, reviews, payment records and verification documents straight away. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
- Flutterwave keeps its own record of each payment, as financial law requires of it.
- Messages you sent are deleted for the other person too.
- Crash and usage data: up to 14 months.

## 7. Your rights
Under Ugandan law you may:
- ask for a copy of your personal data;
- ask us to correct wrong data (you can also edit your profile in the app);
- ask us to delete your data;
- object to some uses of your data;
- complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) at pdpo.go.ug.

## 8. Deleting your account
- **In the app:** Profile, then Settings, then Delete account.
- **Without the app:** use our deletion request page at [DELETION LINK], or email [PRIVACY EMAIL] from the address on your account.

## 9. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database rules make sure each user can only change their own data. CVs and verification documents are kept in private storage that only the right people can open. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and never share sign-in codes.

## 10. Children
Wide-Mart is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is important.
